FAQs

A few things you might be wondering.

Straight answers about the software, your team and getting started.

Can I design systems and produce take-offs in Compliar?

Yes. Premium seats include projects, take-offs and pricing from your quantities. Design includes everything in Premium and adds CAD drawings, zone plans, as-fitted plans, wiring diagrams and schematics for your installation and handover documentation. Explore design and take-offs.

Does Compliar cover handover packs and fire safety logbooks?

Compliar supports the records that carry a job through handover: certificates and reports, plus zone plans and as-fitted drawings with a Design seat. Digital fire safety logbooks are built into the app from Lite, with customer portal access for sites where the logbook is enabled. You can create a user-customised, printable QR code for the site. Display it on site so people can scan it to open the fire safety logbook. Explore handover packs or see fire safety logbooks.

How much does Compliar cost?

Compliar is priced per person, by seat: Lite £25, Pro £35, Premium £40 and Design £50 per user per month, plus VAT. Pay yearly and you get two months free. You can mix seats in one company, there is no minimum number of seats, and every account starts with a 30-day free trial, no card required.

What does each seat include?

Lite works in the field or at a desk: calendar, jobs, customers, sites and assets, certificates, defects, quotes, reports and timesheets, with access from a phone or desk. Lite is limited to those features. Pro adds more office tools: maintenance plans, invoicing and credit control, rates, stock, purchase orders and RAMS. Premium adds projects and tenders, take-offs, job costing, advanced reports and your own certificate templates. Design adds CAD drawings, zone plans and as-fitted plans. Fire safety logbooks are included from Lite. Each seat includes everything in the one below it.

What disciplines does Compliar cover?

19 disciplines across fire safety and protection, security and electrical. Coverage includes fire alarms, aspirating detection, emergency lighting, evacuation alerts, extinguishers, gas suppression, sprinklers, risers, hydrants and fire risk assessments (FRAs); intruder alarms, CCTV and access control; electrical installation, inspection and testing, EV charging and PAT testing. Manage design, pricing, installation, commissioning and maintenance, with certificates and reports linked to the work. Explore the trades.

Do the certificates follow British Standards?

Compliar's certificate templates are built to follow the relevant British Standards for each discipline, and the template for each discipline names the standard it follows. They include BS 5839-1 (fire alarms), PD 6662 & BS EN 50131 (intruder alarms), BS EN IEC 62676-4 (CCTV), BS 5266-1 (emergency lighting) and BS 7671 (electrical installations). The engineer who signs a certificate remains responsible for its content.

Is there a contract or minimum commitment?

No. Every account starts with a 30-day free trial, no card required, and there is no minimum term or minimum number of seats.

Can I use different seats in one company?

Yes. Each person has their own seat, so you can put field engineers on Lite, the office on Pro, an estimator on Premium or Design, and change a person's seat later.

How does Compliar help with safe working?

RAMS — risk assessments and method statements — is included with Pro seats and above. Create one from a job using discipline-specific templates, or start from scratch. Review the hazards and methods for the actual site and task, then sign off and issue a PDF. It helps your team plan work with the safety of engineers, clients and others on site in mind. Explore RAMS.

Can I price a whole project, not just a job?

Yes. Projects & tenders let you start from a prospect or an existing customer site, split the work into areas, and build a bill of quantities with parts, labour hours and waste at your target margin. Projects are numbered separately from your day-to-day jobs.

Is there a mobile app?

The field app runs in your phone's browser, so there is nothing to download from an app store. Technicians get a simplified view built for site work: status and timer, directions, parts, billable items and the certificates a job needs, without the office clutter. It needs a mobile signal on site; an offline mode is in testing and is not switched on for customers yet.

Who can see my data?

Each company's data is kept separate in the database itself, so one customer of Compliar can never see another's. Your own customers only see what you give them: a portal login can be limited to a whole account or a single site.
