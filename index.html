Why Compliar exists

Built by someone who was doing the job, not selling to it.

Compliar was built by someone who has spent 20 years in electrical, fire and security — leading teams through audits, taking fire & security companies through compliance and accreditation, and working with plenty of different software in the field and at the desk. None of it ever covered everything the job needed. So Compliar started as a working tool inside a small fire, security & electrical company, shaped around how a 4–6 person team actually gets through a week: a technician checking in on site, filling in a real certificate, and a quote or invoice going out the same day.

The platforms that come closest are built and priced for much bigger companies, out of reach for the small and medium-sized businesses that make up most of the industry. Compliar covers every part of running a job and a business in this trade — scheduling, certification across electrical, life and property safety in nineteen disciplines, quoting, invoicing, projects, job costing, reporting, assets and stock — at a price that fits the size of company most of the industry actually is. You pay per person, and each person's seat includes what their job needs. No module you'll never switch on, and no minimum number of seats.

Site compliance

Every site, red, amber or green — at a glance

Compliar scores each site from its real records — failed assets, open defects and maintenance plans past due — and takes the worst of its systems. The colour shows on the dashboard, the sites list and every customer, and your office team gets an in-app alert when a site turns red.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask

How much does Compliar cost?

Compliar is priced per person, by seat: Lite £20, Pro £30, Premium £38 and Design £49 per user per month, plus VAT. Pay yearly and you get two months free. You can mix seats in one company, there is no minimum number of seats, and every account starts with a 30-day free trial, no card required.

What does each seat include?

Lite is for field engineers: calendar, jobs, customers, sites and assets, certificates, defects, quotes, reports and timesheets, with the field app. Pro adds the office desk: maintenance plans, invoicing and credit control, rates, stock, purchase orders and RAMS. Premium adds projects and tenders, job costing, advanced reports and your own certificate templates. Design adds drawings and take-offs. Each seat includes everything in the one below it.

What disciplines does Compliar cover?

19 disciplines across fire, security and electrical — fire alarm, intruder alarm, CCTV, access control, emergency lighting, electrical installation, EV charging, PAT testing and more, all in one system.

Do the certificates follow British Standards?

Compliar's certificate templates are built to follow the relevant British Standards for each discipline, and each one names the standard it follows. They include BS 5839-1 (fire alarms), PD 6662 & BS EN 50131 (intruder alarms), BS EN 62676-4 (CCTV), BS 5266-1 (emergency lighting) and BS 7671 (electrical installations). The engineer who signs a certificate remains responsible for its content.

Is there a contract or minimum commitment?

No. Every account starts with a 30-day free trial, no card required, and there is no minimum term or minimum number of seats.

Can I use different seats in one company?

Yes. Each person has their own seat, so you can put field engineers on Lite, the office on Pro, an estimator on Premium or Design, and change a person's seat later.

Is RAMS (risk assessments) available yet?

Yes — RAMS is live. Create one straight from a job and it pulls in hazards and a method statement from ready-made templates across every discipline, or start from a blank RAMS for anything bespoke. Each one is signed off and issued as a PDF.

Can I price a whole project, not just a job?

Yes. Projects & tenders let you start from a prospect or an existing customer site, split the work into areas, and build a bill of quantities with parts, labour hours and waste at your target margin. Projects are numbered separately from your day-to-day jobs.

Is there a mobile app?

The field app runs in your phone's browser, so there is nothing to download from an app store. Technicians get a simplified view built for site work: status and timer, directions, parts, billable items and the certificates a job needs, without the office clutter. It needs a mobile signal on site; an offline mode is in testing and is not switched on for customers yet.

Who can see my data?

Each company's data is kept separate in the database itself, so one customer of Compliar can never see another's. Your own customers only see what you give them: a portal login can be limited to a whole account or a single site.
